How this works (30-second version)

Each card is one of the hard bits. The video starts on the teacher's explanation and loops it automatically. Slow it down, zoom in on the hand you need, and let Ladder speed it up a little each time it loops until you're at full speed. Anything you change (loop points, zoom, speed) is saved in this browser.

Shortcuts work on the highlighted card: Space play/pause · [ ] slower/faster · L loop on/off · A/B set loop start/end to now · Z cycle zoom (full → fret hand → pick hand) · M mirror · F full screen · ←/→ jump 2s · R back to loop start

The YouTube controls are hidden so zooming stays clean. Use the buttons below each video instead. When you're zoomed in, drag on the video to move the view. Each zoom preset remembers your adjustment for that clip.

The videos belong to their creators and play through YouTube's official embedded player (ads and creator credit stay in). Loop times came from each lesson's YouTube chapters and captions and may be off by a second or two. Anything marked "approx — adjust" is a best guess, so fix it with the A/B buttons. Tab excerpts are short practice passages transcribed from the teachers' spoken instructions (see the source note on each card).
